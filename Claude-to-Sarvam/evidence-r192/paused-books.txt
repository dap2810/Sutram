+Archived edition (R157, 9 November 2026). This book describes Sutram as it was at v294 (3 November 2026). It is no longer kept up to date: development is in Sanskrit only, and the language has changed since - among other things, this edition has no chapter on memory (kosh, mandapa, slices) and does not teach reading a line of text (vartani_grahan, vartani_tak_ank). One sentence in it is known to be false (R192, 11 November 2026): the section "Descending Loops" says a chakra counts down by itself whenever the start is greater than the end. That is true only when both ends are written as numbers (chakra i = 5 tak 1); with a variable at either end the loop counts up, and runs no times at all, with no message, when the start is past the end. To count down from a variable use yavat - see "Descending Loops" in the English edition. The text of this edition is left as it was. The current books are the English and Sanskrit editions.इदं संस्करणं v294-काले यथा सूत्रम् आसीत् तथा वर्णयति, अद्यतनं न क्रियते। वर्तमाने ग्रन्थौ: आङ्ग्ल-संस्करणं संस्कृत-संस्करणं च।
