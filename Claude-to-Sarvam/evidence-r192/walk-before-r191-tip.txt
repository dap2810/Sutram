== Chapter 1, "What you need", as printed
   What you need: a Linux x86-64 system (or WSL on Windows). No other
   dependency — not even a C compiler. The compiler is a single self-contained binary.

== Chapter 2, the first program, as printed
   mukhya()
       likha("Namaste, Sutram!\n")
   -- the book says the student will see:
   $ sutram namaste.sm namaste.bin
   Siddh: 97 baait sankalit
   $ ./namaste.bin
   Namaste, Sutram!
   -- the student sees:
   $ sutram namaste.sm namaste.bin
     Siddh: 112 baait sankalit
   $ ./namaste.bin   typed ''           ->  'Namaste, Sutram!\n'
