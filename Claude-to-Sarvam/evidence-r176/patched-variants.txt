bytes changed by hand - not compiler builds:
  t_small.v318                      compiled by v318 (no zeroing at the exit)
  t_small.zero                      compiled with the zeroing at the exit (R175's code; v320)
  t_small.exit_fixed_only           the same, "jbe .none" made "jmp .none": the exit's instructions run, its loop does not
  t_small.exit_zero_no_alloc_zero   the same as t_small.zero, with the zeroing loop inside the block's nirmmita removed
                                    (its store made a no-op, its backward jump removed)
  t_small.neither                   both removed
  noblock.v319                      the same loop with no mandapa at all (each nirmmita(64) stays)
