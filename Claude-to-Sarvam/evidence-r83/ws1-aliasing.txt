Reading: a1 - nothing else allocated: on Linux k grows IN PLACE (R65: the newest block extends), a and k stay one
buffer (a == k is 1, k[0] = 7 shows through a). On PE and in the guard-page build there is no in-place growth: they
split. a2 - one nirmmita(64) in between: they split on Linux too; a write through a (99) never reaches k. a3 - passed
as a kosh, the stale name reports length 2, the real one 3. b1 - R42's 'push through the alias grows the original'
holds only without growth: after a growing push through w, kosh_len(v) is 1 and kosh_len(w) 2.

Is anything recycled? No: the compiler emits no munmap / VirtualFree anywhere (grep of src/ and win/); the mandapa
rewind moves only the scratch cursor, and a kosh is never on the scratch side (R82). The old buffer stays mapped and
owned: a stale read or write, not a use-after-free. The guard-page build (every block its own mapping, nothing
unmapped) runs all of these without a fault.
