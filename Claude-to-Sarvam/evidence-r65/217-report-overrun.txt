examples/217_r12_report.sm: the one program of 248 whose output changed (it has no golden: NO-EXPECTATION).

  vitti koshtha = nirmmita(32)        # 32 bytes = 4 qword cells
  ...
  pad_vam(koshtha, cnt, 6, 32)        # lib/vakyank.smlib: 'Caller must allocate >= width cells' - width 6 = 48 bytes
  vitti row = nirmmita(64)            # allocated right after koshtha

pad_vam writes cells 4 and 5 of koshtha, 16 bytes past its 32-byte block. With one mmap per nirmmita those bytes
were the unused rest of koshtha's own page. In the arena they are the first 16 bytes of row, which the program
then prints: the 'Padded scores' lines read '     5' where they read '    85'.

Old (v262) output lines 15-22:  85 92 78 95 88 76 90 84 (right-aligned in 6)
New (v263) output lines 15-22:   5  2  8  5  8  6  0  4
Both runs are deterministic. The example's bug is exposed, not caused; it is reported, not fixed (the brief).
