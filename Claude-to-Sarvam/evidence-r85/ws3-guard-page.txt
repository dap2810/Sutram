The stale name reads its OLD block's last cell (111); one cell further is the guard page (SIGSEGV). So the old block is a LIVE, mapped allocation with its guard in place, not a freed one: these are
stale handles reading a live block. Nothing frees a block in any build: no munmap / VirtualFree anywhere in src/.
