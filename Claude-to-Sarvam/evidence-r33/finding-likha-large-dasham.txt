Found while fuzzing the FFI, and NOT an FFI defect: likha prints a large dasham inexactly. The ordinary path, Linux, compiler v242 (and v241):

  mukhya() {
      likha(1870271503745.0)
      likha(160935754977.75)
      likha(100000000.5)
      likha(4294967296.25)
  }
prints:
  1870271503744.999936
  160935754977.750016
  100000000.500000
  4294967296.250000
C's printf("%f") gives 1870271503745.000000 and 160935754977.750000 for the first two. The values are exact
binary64 numbers; only the printing loses digits, from about 1e11 up. tools/fuzz_ffi.py keeps printed dasham below 1e8.
