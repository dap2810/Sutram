**Console rendering (6′).** I ran the same three commands in a real classic console window
(`conhost.exe cmd.exe`, active code page 437) and screenshotted only that window: `win-run-2026-10-21/f8/f8-console-conhost.png`.
The right characters appear in the console (so the console write path is Unicode-correct, even under cp 437),
but **the console does not shape complex scripts**. In `भाषा पैक मिला` the vowel signs sit in separate cells with
gaps, and `सिद्धम्` and `सङ्कलिताः` do not form their conjuncts. Tamil is readable but also spaced out. A
reader can make out the text, but it is not what a Hindi, Sanskrit or Tamil reader expects to see. This is the
classic console's renderer, not Sutram's bytes. **Windows Terminal, the Windows 11 default, was NOT-RUN:
`wt.exe` is not on PATH from this session.** It should be checked separately.
